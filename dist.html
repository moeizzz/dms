1. FOUNDATION & HIERARCHY SETUP

Base System ki base. Business rules define ki jaati hain.

  • Multi-Company & Multi-Branch: Ek hi software mein multiple distribution companies ya zones manage karna (e.g., North Zone, South Zone).
  • Warehouse Network: Central Godown, Sub-Warehouses, aur C&F (Carrying & Forwarding) agents ka management.
  • Financial Year & Taxation: Dynamic GST/VAT slabs, HSN/SAC codes, aur TDS deduction rules.
  • User Roles (RBAC): Distributor Owner, Warehouse Manager, Sales Officer, Accounts Clerk, Delivery Boy – har ek ke alag rights.

2. PRODUCT & PRICING CATALOG (PIM)

Core Distribution ka dil. Yahan products define hote hain.

  • Multi-level Categories: Example: Beverages → Cold Drinks → 500ml PET.
  • Multiple Units (UoM): Pcs, Box (12 Pcs), Carton (48 Pcs). System auto-calculate karega conversion.
  • Distributor Pricing Engine:
    ▪ Primary Rate: Company se Distributor ko jo rate milta hai.
    ▪ Secondary Rate: Distributor retailer ko jo rate deta hai.
    ▪ Scheme Management: Buy 10 Get 1 Free, Extra Discount on bulk, ya Credit Note schemes.
  • Barcodes: Product aur Batch level pe barcode generate karna (EAN-13 / QR).

3. PROCUREMENT & INWARD SUPPLY CHAIN

Inward Company se maal aana – Primary Sales

  • Purchase Order (PO): Auto-generate ho jab stock reorder level se neechay chala jaye.
  • GRN (Goods Receipt Note): Godown pe maal aane par Quality Check (QC). Kuch maal reject ho sakta hai, kuch accepted.
  • Supplier Return (RMA): Kharab maal wapas bhejna aur Debit Note generate karna.
  • Landing Cost: Freight, Insurance, aur handling charges ko product cost mein add karna.

4. WAREHOUSE & INVENTORY OPS (WMS)

Warehouse Godown ke andar ka complete management

  • Bin/Rack Management: Godown ke andar specific racks define karna (Aisle-1, Rack-3, Shelf-2).
  • Batch & Expiry (FEFO/FIFO): Pehle Expiry wala maal pehle nikale (Pharma/FMCG ke liye zaroori).
  • Stock Transfers: Ek warehouse se doosre warehouse maal bhejna (with In-transit tracking).
  • Cycle Count: Bina godown band kiye physical stock count karna aur system se match karna.
  • Van Stock: Salesman ki gadi (Van) mein kitna maal rakha hai, uska alag inventory.

5. DEALER & RETAILER MANAGEMENT (CRM)

CRM B2B customers ka data

  • Customer 360° View: Retailer ka complete history – kya khareeda, kitna return kiya, kitna payment baaki hai.
  • Credit Limit Management: Har retailer ke liye max credit limit set karna. Limit cross ho toh order book na ho.
  • Beat/Route Planning: Salesman ko daily kis route pe jaana hai, kis retailer ko visit karna hai (Google Maps integration).
  • Complaint Logging: Retailer ki complaint (late delivery, damaged product) ko track karna aur SLA ke hisaab se resolve karna.

6. SALES & SECONDARY SALES

Outward Distributor se Retailer tak bechna

  • Quotation & Order Booking: Salesman retailer ke yahan jaakar order book kare (Offline mode bhi chalna chahiye).
  • Van Sales (D2R): Gadi mein hi stock rakhein, retailer ko maal dein aur turant invoice print karein (Handheld device).
  • Sales Invoice: Tax invoice generate hona, jisme MRP, Discount, aur Net Amount clear ho.
  • Sales Return: Retailer kharab maal wapas kare toh Credit Note issue karna aur stock mein wapas add karna (as damaged grade).
  • Indent Management: Retailer khud mobile app ya portal ke through indent (demand) daale.

7. LAST-MILE DELIVERY LOGISTICS

Logistics Gadi se retailer tak pahunchana

  • Route Optimization: Sab se chhota aur fuel-efficient route suggest karna.
  • Driver App: Delivery boy ke phone pe app. GPS tracking, delivery status update.
  • Proof of Delivery (POD): Retailer ka digital signature, photo, aur OTP lena delivery confirm karne ke liye.
  • Fleet Management: Vehicles ka maintenance record, diesel expense, aur challan tracking.

8. ACCOUNTING & FINANCIAL RECONCILIATION

Finance Paise ka hisaab

  • Customer Ledger: Har retailer ka individual khata (Debit/Credit).
  • Supplier Ledger: Har company/supplier ka khata.
  • Bank Reconciliation: Bank statement upload karke system auto-match karega payments.
  • Expense Management: Petty cash, Office expenses, aur Staff salaries ka entry.
  • Financial Statements: Profit & Loss (P&L), Balance Sheet, aur Cash Flow statement generate karna.
  • Primary Sales Settlement: Company se commission/scheme ka paisa kab aana hai uska tracking.

9. AUTOMATION & EARLY WARNINGS

Automation Bina manual intervention ke kaam ho

  • Auto Reorder Alerts: Stock low ho toh Purchase Order automatically generate ho ya Manager ko alert jaye (SMS/Email).
  • Overdue Payment Reminders: Jis retailer ka payment 15 din se zyada overdue hai, usko auto reminder.
  • Approval Workflows: Agar kisi retailer ko extra discount dena hai toh Manager ki approval zaroori ho.
  • Automated Backups: Har raat system ka automatic backup cloud pe store hona.

10. BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE (BI) & DASHBOARDS

Analytics Decision making ke liye data

  • Owner Dashboard: Today's Sales, Pending Payments, Top Selling Products, aur Regional Performance.
  • Warehouse Dashboard: Current Stock Value, Expiry Alerts, aur Space Utilization.
  • Salesman Dashboard: Target vs Achievement, Today's collections.
  • Drill-down Reports: Jaise "North Zone ki sales kyun kam hui" – click karte hi raw data saamne aa jaye.

11. MOBILE APPS & INTEGRATION

Ecosystem Sab ko unki device pe chahiye

  • Sales Rep App: Offline order booking, payment collection, visit tracking, catalog display.
  • Warehouse App: Barcode scanner se Pick/Pack karna, GRN entry.
  • Retailer App: Retailer khud apna order place kare, payment history dekhe, scheme check kare.
  • Integrations: Tally/SAP/QuickBooks se data sync, aur E-commerce platforms (Amazon/Shopify) se order import.

12. TESTING, TRAINING & GO-LIVE

Launch System launch ka safar

  • UAT (User Acceptance Testing): Real data pe system check karna with 5-10 retailers.
  • Data Migration: Old Excel/manual data ko system mein shift karna.
  • Role-based Training: Accounts wale ko sirf accounts sikhana, warehouse staff ko sirf WMS.
  • Phased Go-Live: Pehle 1 branch pe system chalao, phir sare branches pe rollout karo.
  • Support SLA: Go-Live ke baad 3 months free bug-fixing aur support.
